What Is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which criminals pretend to be trustworthy people or organizations to trick people into revealing sensitive information or opening malicious links.

Common Types

  • Email phishing: Fraudulent emails.
  • Spear phishing: Targeted messages aimed at a particular person or organization.
  • Smishing: Phishing through SMS messages.
  • Vishing: Phishing through phone calls.

How to Recognize Phishing

  • Unexpected messages asking for passwords or payments.
  • Urgent threats that pressure you to act immediately.
  • Sender addresses that do not match the real organization.
  • Unexpected attachments or suspicious links.
  • Spelling mistakes or unusual requests.

Safety tip: Verify requests through a trusted channel before clicking links or sharing information.

Example of a Suspicious Email

From: security@example-suspicious.test

Subject: Urgent! Your account will close!

Your account has a problem. Click the link immediately to prevent it from being closed.

Training link: [Fictional link omitted]

Red Flags

  • Creates fear and urgency.
  • Uses an unfamiliar sender address.
  • Pressures the recipient to click without verifying.

This is a fictional training example. Do not click unexpected links in real messages.

How to Stay Safe

  • Check the sender's full email address.
  • Visit official websites directly instead of using unexpected links.
  • Enable multifactor authentication (MFA).
  • Keep software and security tools updated.
  • Report suspicious messages to your IT or security team.
